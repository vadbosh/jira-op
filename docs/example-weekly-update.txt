This is one accepted shape — the compact one, and the one this team sends. The
fuller variant is equally correct: section titles in sentence case, the ticket
title quoted after the key, a severity marker on a risk, an Owner on each Next
Week item. Match whatever the team is already sending; do not convert a report
from one shape to the other for its own sake.

What varies between the two shapes is only presentation: capitalisation of the
section titles, whether the ticket title is quoted, whether items are grouped
under a workstream line, where Owner sits, and whether risks carry 🔴/🟡.

What never varies, in either shape: Impact: and Mitigation: on a risk, Why: and
Decision makers: on a decision, two sentences per item, no lists of components
or versions, no tracker bookkeeping, plain text, and the long-ticket rule below.

Note what the example does and does not contain:

- The header carries the engineer's name, taken from the token owner's Jira
  profile, and one board line. No reporting-window line: the window is in the
  file name.
- Section titles are in Title Case, exactly as written above, and the markers
  are emoji because the destination renders them.
- What Went Well is one flat list, with no headings inside the section. An item
  is the key, an em dash and the outcome — no quoted ticket title, and no label.
  Grouping under a workstream line happens only when asked for, and the group is
  then named after the work, never after the age of its tickets.
- Work with no ticket — a meeting, a review, coordination — is a plain line in
  the same list.
- Two of the three tickets recorded no transition that week. They are here
  because they were in progress during the window — status WAS "In Progress"
  DURING is the list, and a status change is not required.
- PROJ-707 and PROJ-243 run for months. An empty changelog on such a ticket is
  not an empty week: the work follows the plan written inside the ticket, so the
  item says so. The phrase "no movement this week" is never written. A ticket
  nobody touched is one line under Next Week instead.
- A risk states the risk, then Impact: and Mitigation:, each one sentence. No
  severity markers.
- A decision states what was decided, then Why: and Decision makers:.
- Next Week opens with a single Owner: line for the section, then the committed
  items — no per-item owner unless the section mixes owners.
- Two sentences per item, and no lists of components, versions or paths — the
  count and the shape, with the detail left in the ticket.
- Nothing about ticket age, sprint carry-overs, empty fields or missing
  comments. That is the tracker's bookkeeping, not the week's work.
- No Markdown. Plain text wrapped near 80 characters.
- The file lands in the project working directory as
  weekly-update-<start>_<end>.txt, and the assistant answers with the path
  alone.
